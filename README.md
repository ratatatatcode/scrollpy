# Voice & Clap-Controlled Mouse and Arrow Keys
If you're feeling lazy, watching videos, or gaming with your hands full—this tool is your best friend. Control scrolling and arrow keys without touching the keyboard or mouse. Just speak or clap.

Honestly, this is just too random and very me.
I was bored, kinda lazy, multitasking between games and social media—so I thought,
"Why not control the mouse and arrow keys with just my voice or a clap?"
